What We Do

Custom Wall Printing Without Wallpaper or Vinyl

Direct-to-wall printing is an advanced technology that prints high-resolution artwork, murals, and branding directly onto your wall surface using UV-cured eco-friendly inks. Unlike wallpaper or vinyl, there are no seams, no adhesives, and no peeling edges — the result is a seamless, permanent finish that becomes part of the wall itself.

Our German-engineered vertical printer travels up to 4 metres tall and can print on virtually any flat surface — plasterboard (Gyprock), brick, concrete, glass, timber, metal, and more. The ink cures instantly under UV light, so there’s no wait time, no chemical odour, and no downtime for your home or business.

Wall Envy is a proudly family-owned business based on the Central Coast, serving the Central Coast, Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the broader Hunter Region. We bring together carpentry expertise, interior design sensibility, and cutting-edge printing technology to deliver truly extraordinary results.

Simple Process

From Vision to Masterpiece

Four clean steps — no mess, no downtime, no surprises.

1

Design & Consultation

We collaborate with you on artwork, scale, and colour. Digital mockups provided before any printing begins.

2

Preparation & Setup

Minimal site prep. Our vertical printer is set up on-site, laser-calibrating to your wall’s exact height — up to 4 metres.

3

The Print

Eco-friendly, UV-cured ink bonds directly to your surface in real-time. Dry the second it’s printed. No odour, no waiting.

4

Final Reveal

We pack up immediately. Zero cleanup, no curing time. Ready for compliments — and social media tags — right away.
