Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about direct-to-wall printing.

Wall printing is an innovative technology that allows us to print high-quality, intricate designs directly onto your walls. It's a new alternative to traditional methods like painting or wallpapering.
The process begins with a consultation where we discuss your ideas, preferences, and specifics of the space. Then our designers create high-resolution art. We use advanced wall printing machines to translate the design onto your wall with meticulous attention to detail.
Yes, we can print on a diverse range of surfaces, including Sheetrock (drywall), Plaster Walls, Brick Walls, Glass Walls, Metal Walls, and Wood Walls. This also includes floors. We do not however print on ceilings.
We can print a virtually unlimited range of designs, from subtle patterns to vibrant, large-scale murals.
Absolutely! We work closely with each client to understand their vision, and we can use any high-resolution image you provide. If the image is not high resolution, our design team can try and enhance the image for your size requirements. Vector art can be set to any size you want.
The duration of the process depends on the size of the wall and the complexity of the design. We can provide a more accurate estimate after the initial consultation.
No, wall printing does not cause any damage to the wall. If you want to change or remove the design, all you have to do is simply paint right over it.
The cost is determined based on several factors, including the travel & setup time, size of the print, the complexity of the design & print job, and the type of wall surface.
Absolutely! Our skilled designers can work with you to come up with a design that suits your space and personal style. Our team will send you proofs which you will provide an approval on before we start any work.
The lead time can vary depending on our current schedule and the size of your project. We are taking on projects now and we can consult with you on scheduling.
With proper care, a wall print can last for many years. Typically, indoor prints can last 12 years and outdoor 5 years. For outdoor prints we suggest adding a protective layer of clear coat which would extend the lifespan outdoors.
