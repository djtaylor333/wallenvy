Our Story

About Wall Envy

A family-owned business where carpentry craftsmanship meets interior design expertise.

Who We Are

Craftsmanship Meets Creative Vision

Wall Envy is a family-owned direct-to-wall printing business serving the Central Coast and Hunter regions of NSW, Australia. We combine two powerful professional backgrounds — Nicole’s eye for interior design and our team’s decades of professional carpentry and surface expertise — to deliver wall transformations that are truly one of a kind.

We’re not just a printing company. We’re craftspeople who understand surfaces, spaces, and the power of a well-executed design. Every project we take on is backed by a genuine commitment to quality, from the initial consultation right through to the final reveal.

The People Behind the Print

🌟

Interior Design & Client Consulting

Nicole brings a background in interior styling and design, working directly with clients to understand their vision, curate the perfect artwork, select colour palettes, and ensure every design fits seamlessly within the existing space. She doesn’t just take a brief — she helps shape it into something exceptional.

🔨

Surface Preparation & Precision Printing

Backed by years of professional experience at J. Yates Carpentry, our team ensures every surface is perfectly prepared, patched, and primed before a single drop of ink is applied. A premium print requires a flawless foundation — and our carpentry expertise means we deliver exactly that, every time.

Our Technology

German-Engineered UV Precision

🌏

Direct-to-Surface Printing

Our advanced vertical printer attaches directly to your wall and prints with laser-accurate alignment — accommodating heights of up to 4 metres.

⚡

Instant UV Cure

Ink is cured with UV light the moment it leaves the print head. Dry instantly, no odour, no off-gassing — safe for homes, schools, and clinics immediately after we leave.

🌿

Eco-Friendly Inks

German-engineered, low-VOC, non-toxic inks that bond permanently to the surface. No peeling, no seams, no harmful chemicals — ever.

🏗

Any Surface

Plasterboard, brick, glass, concrete, timber, metal, tiles — if it’s vertical, there’s an excellent chance we can print on it.
